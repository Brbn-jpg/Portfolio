# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Technical recruiters, engineering managers, potential collaborators, and fellow developers evaluating Jakub Kuźnicki's experience, skills, and personal projects.

## Product Purpose

Present Jakub's backend, AI, infrastructure, and full-stack work in a concise portfolio that makes technical breadth, professional experience, and project outcomes easy to assess.

## Brand Personality

Technical, direct, and curious. The interface should feel like a polished developer workspace: confident and personal without becoming corporate or overly decorative.

## Anti-references

Generic light SaaS landing pages, inflated marketing copy, decorative effects that compete with project information, and portfolio templates that hide technical substance behind presentation.

## Design Principles

- Put concrete work and technical evidence before self-promotion.
- Preserve the distinctive terminal-inspired voice across pages.
- Keep project information concise, scannable, and honest.
- Use hierarchy to make the strongest and most current work visible first.
- Add personality through purposeful details rather than visual noise.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain semantic HTML, keyboard-accessible links and controls, visible focus states, readable contrast, descriptive image alternatives, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion-friendly behavior.
